This read-only research axis attempted to close the proof-domain mismatch exposed
by SCORE-MASTER-PORTFOLIO-096. The complete state carries two active patrol
positions/fuels, lifetime score state, and the previous two exact own road
footprints; frozen opponent mass and the official players-times-threshold rule
derive each future Smooth/Busy/Jammed status.

Three configuration attempts were rejected by the public parser before any score:
spots on mountain cells, starts on non-plain/spot cells, and daySteps below the
published 8x8 bound. The final adapter uses valid plain starts and 16--18 steps.

The first exact formulation stored 64-cell integer footprint vectors in every
node/key and reached about 6.8 GB private allocation before completion. The same
semantics were compressed to only the two road offsets, reducing the comparable
stage to about 2.3 GB. A second exact quotient stored each road count as a byte
and saturated it at `players * jammedThreshold`: counts above that value are
observationally identical for next-day status and disappear after the two-day
window. Componentwise traffic dominance was also enabled only when positions
and fuels were identical and the challenger had no larger recent load.

Even after these exact reductions, one low-fuel development fixture reached
about 5.8 GB private allocation before producing a score. The process was stopped
to protect the shared local machine. No fixture completed, no result hash exists,
and the holdout never opened. Local runtime is not a production performance
claim; this is only evidence that this complete oracle representation is not a
practical research instrument on the current host.

Verdict: infrastructure-inconclusive, source retained only as research scaffolding.
Reopen only with a mathematically exact state quotient or external proof host
whose bounded memory is preregistered. Do not reduce horizon, traffic history,
threshold semantics or validation to force completion.
