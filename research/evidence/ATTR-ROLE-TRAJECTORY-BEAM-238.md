## Verdict

Rejected as a production mechanism and fully reverted. Multi-trajectory
information is real, but selecting its realized maximum has an unbounded
same-tanker-count identity tail (-29 in the frozen split). No sealed holdout was
created or opened. The source after closure contains no trajectory-beam switch.

Do not reopen this mechanism by changing beam width, work split, fuel threshold,
tanker-count condition or a seed/family dispatcher. A successor needs a new
state-coupled certificate that bounds same-count identity downside before it may
consume additional trajectory evidence; otherwise retain canonical `18ecdd3`.
