# ATTR-EXACT-FUEL-PREVALENCE-177

Date: 2026-08-20  
Parent: `828ea78`  
Status: closed — method-intractable and non-general  

## Question

Experiment 175 proved one exact public-information counterexample: robust oracle
`6/12/12` versus unchanged HEAD `6/11/11` on low-fuel balanced seed `1720000`.
Experiment 176 reproduced that score causally but broad nonterminal fuel-exact
activation regressed fresh general development at `1/5/6` W/T/L. Before any
new source design, determine whether the exact fuel-preserving gap repeats as a
public structural class or is an isolated witness.

## Method

Use the unchanged experiment-175 oracle and its proved equal-terminal-cell,
equal-exact-saturated-footprint max-node dominance quotient. Run the eight
still-unopened development rows from the frozen 175 manifest:

`1720100`, `1720200`, `1721000`, `1721100`, `1721200`, `1722000`, `1722100`,
`1722200`.

Each row is a complete four-day public max-own/min-legal-opponent solve with
`--boundary-dominance 1`, followed by unchanged HEAD under the registered causal
opponent policies. Run rows independently and pull each completed log off the
Spot VM immediately. The 27 holdout rows remain sealed.

## Development results in progress

- Fresh seed `1721000` (`adversarial-threshold`, low fuel) completed exactly:
  robust oracle `5/11/11`, unchanged HEAD `5/10/11` under each of
  `maximum-dwell`, `minimum-dwell` and `status-toggle`. This is `3/0/0` oracle
  W/T/L, all at tier 2 with gain `+1`, with both plans valid and zero HEAD
  deadline-limited days. The solve visited `6,203,765` memo states and
  `604,210,103` legal transitions. This proves the gap is not unique to
  consumed balanced seed `1720000`, but by itself does not yet establish a
  safe production discriminator or reopen rejected candidate 176.
- Fresh seed `1722000` (`adversarial-terminal`, low fuel) also completed as an
  oracle win under all three policies: oracle `5/11/12`, HEAD `5/11/11`, hence
  `3/0/0` W/T/L at tier 3 with gain `+1`, zero invalid and zero deadline-limited
  HEAD days. Its witness is directionally different from 175/176: on day 1 the
  oracle deliberately takes only `2/2/2`, stays at terminal fuel `10` and
  reaches `5/11/12` with a final-day burst; HEAD takes `5/5/5`, ends day 1 at
  fuel `3` and cannot recover the twelfth serving. Therefore the fresh gaps are
  already inconsistent with a single front-load-fuel rule. Any safe successor
  must represent the common horizon value exactly enough to choose between
  preservation and expenditure; seed/family routing or reopening 176 remains
  forbidden.

## Parallel attribution while larger exact rows run

- The three completed exact gaps now span balanced (`1720000`), threshold
  (`1721000`) and terminal (`1722000`) construction, but all three are low-fuel
  four-day fixtures.  Their policy-stable wins prove a repeated horizon/fuel
  valuation gap; they do not yet provide a production discriminator.
- On `1720000` and `1722000`, unchanged HEAD front-loads current-day score and
  reaches the later days with materially less fuel, while the exact policy
  delays some current score and recovers the missing tier on the suffix.  The
  `1721000` completion did not emit per-day detail, so its internal fuel
  direction is not inferred from the final score alone.
- The public predicate `low fuel` is insufficient for a successor: experiment
  176 already produced systematic fresh losses including fuel-tight and
  overnight fixtures.  Until a state-level quantity predicts both the exact
  wins and those losses, broad nonterminal exact activation, F0 reservation and
  bound-closed takeover remain closed.
- The admissible next attribution is therefore read-only: compare marginal
  reachable-brand value of retained fuel, remaining days/steps, terminal
  reachability and exact traffic state against the 176 loss families.  No
  source candidate, threshold or dispatcher is authorized by the current
  sample.

The missing detailed trace for fresh threshold seed `1721000` was subsequently
reconstructed with the accepted quotient-178 oracle.  Under every causal policy
the day sequence is identical.  On day 1 the oracle accepts only `2/2` and ends
at cell 17 with fuel 9, while HEAD front-loads `5/6`, ends at cell 19 with fuel
2 and leaves one road stay.  The oracle then scores `3/3` on each of days 2--4;
HEAD scores `2/2`, `2/2`, `1/1`.  Both finish at cell 19 with fuel 0, but the
oracle reaches `5/11/11` versus HEAD `5/10/11`.  This closes the previously
unknown direction: all three completed low-fuel wins are pre-terminal fuel-value
errors caused by current-score front-loading, not merely final-score coincidences.

That common behavior still does not authorize the rejected 176 mechanism.  All
three exact fixtures deliberately contain one genuinely active patrol and two
isolated controls; 176 broadened exact fuel work into interacting multi-agent
general fixtures and regressed six of twelve.  A possible public discriminator
is therefore exact decomposition into an independently reachable active patrol
component, not `low fuel`.  It remains a hypothesis only: shared brand identity,
stock, own/opponent traffic and team-master coupling must all be proved absent or
represented exactly before such a component can select a production plan.  A
guard matching the registered one-active fixture shape without this proof would
be overfit and is forbidden.

## Final feasibility and source verdict

At approximately 26 hours, the still-resultless unquotiented jobs had RSS
`12,615,024` KiB (`1721100`) and `12,913,176` KiB (`1721200`), each still using
about one CPU core.  Their logs remained zero bytes and the VM had only
`6,710,013,952` bytes available.  Experiment 178 meanwhile proved an exact
traffic-history quotient across three families, reducing full-horizon memo
states by about 49--50% and transitions by 27--29%.  Continuing the superseded
enumeration until arbitrary OOM therefore no longer has positive research value.

At final capture, `1721100` and `1721200` had elapsed about `97,655` and
`97,302` seconds with RSS `12,943,736` and `13,272,884` KiB; the VM had
`5,913,948` kB `MemAvailable`. After `SIGTERM`, `/usr/bin/time` recorded the
elapsed and maximum-RSS figures above, no oracle output, and memory recovered to
`32,168,852` kB.

The jobs were stopped about 50 minutes before the registered 28-hour cutoff
because the intervening source experiments had already made their outcome
decision-irrelevant:

- 176 is the only implementation that reproduced the multi-day exact prefix,
  but it lost fresh general development `1/5/6`, including four tier-2 losses.
- 180 protected the completed parent and admitted only a bound-closed exact
  sidecar, yet scored `0/2/1` on exact anchors and regressed `1721000` from
  `5/10/11` to `5/9/10`.
- 181 and 182 restricted takeover to the penultimate day and exposed the full
  single-agent resource frontier. They could not reach the oracle witness,
  because that route exists only in the different state created by the oracle's
  day-1 prefix.
- Every completed exact win uses one active patrol plus isolated controls. A
  default/high result from the same artificial family cannot prove a safe
  discriminator for interacting multi-agent competition maps.

The remaining sound implementation is therefore the complete multi-day
closed-loop public minimax. Completed low-fuel rows already require about
`6–8` million memo states and `604–705` million legal transitions; default/high
rows are resultless after 20–27 hours and 11–13 GiB RSS. This feasibility class
cannot fit the production `5000 ms` hard cap, even though quotient 178 is a valid
roughly 49–50% state reduction.

Experiment 177 is closed `closed-method-intractable-nongeneral`. Seeds
`1720200`, `1722100`, `1722200` and all 27 holdout rows remain unopened. No
production source commit is authorized. Reopen only with a genuinely new exact
quotient or closed-loop algorithm that puts the complete public proof inside
`5000 ms` and a non-artificial interacting multi-agent counterexample. Broad
day-1/penultimate exact activation, static certificate takeover, per-day
neighborhoods, fixture guards and further unquotiented brute force are closed.
After the final evidence was recorded, Spot VM `udon-proof-177-0820` and its
auto-delete boot disk were deleted, along with the dedicated recurring monitor.
